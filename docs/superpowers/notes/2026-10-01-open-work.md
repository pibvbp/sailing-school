# Open work before 1.0.0 (as of 2026-10-01)

What is still to do before the 1.0.0 tag, with enough detail to pick each item up cold. It comes from three
reviews of the build of 2026-10-01: a hands-on visual QA pass (stopped part-way), a code review of the overlay
rework, and a code review of the volumetric clouds. Items are ordered by how much a learner would notice.

## Where things stand

Shipped to `main` on 2026-10-01: volumetric clouds, a sea that reflects them over the angle a ruffled surface
scatters, the overlay rework (tag layout, reduced force sets, x-ray with a leeway picture), the Sail lab fixes,
cameras that keep the boat between the panels, spinnaker lines that stay on their corners, the clouds review's
fixes (strip-wise environment bake, CPU mirror tied to the shader), a software-renderer start on the Low tier, and
new README pictures.

All tests are green: typecheck, the Vitest suite, the Playwright suite (also with the software renderer CI uses) and
the real-WebAudio suite.

## Working without a GPU

The look of the sea, sky and boat has to be judged in pictures, and that wants a GPU.

- On a software rasteriser (SwiftShader, llvmpipe: a CI runner, a cloud machine) the app starts on the **Low** tier
  with the painted 2-D clouds (`isSoftwareRenderer` in `src/render/core/renderer.ts`, used in `src/app/App.ts`).
  `app.setQuality('high')` still works there, but the sky was built without volumetric clouds, and a High frame
  takes seconds.
- So the items under "The sea" and "Clouds" below should be tuned on a machine with a GPU. If they must be done
  without one, add a query parameter that keeps the volumetric clouds on a software renderer, expect minutes per
  picture, and step the simulation with `app.setTimeScale` and pauses rather than waiting in real time.
- Everything under "Overlays" and "Still to test" can be done on any machine: it is layout, logic and tests.

Tools: `node scripts/snap.mjs index.html out.png --size 1440x900 --wait 3000 --eval "<js>"` takes one picture;
`node scripts/shots.mjs <recipe.json> <dir>` takes several from one session. The page exposes `window.__app`
(`setMode`, `setCamera`, `setOverlay`, `setWind`, `setTimeOfDay`, `setQuality`, `scenario`, `startLesson`,
`controls`, `sim`), `window.__stats` and `window.__ready`. Do not call `sim.snapshot()` from outside: it drains the
events the app reads.

## 2. Overlays (code review; no GPU needed)

Done on 2026-10-01, with tests:

- **C1.** A reduced force set keeps one scale for as long as the set is unchanged: the largest of the forces it
  actually draws, never decaying (also M6). In lesson 10 step 4 a 15 % smaller heeling force now gives a 15 % shorter
  arrow, two seconds or a minute later, and nothing creeps while paused (`forces.test.ts`).
- **I1.** A tag in place keeps its offset from its anchor (it follows its own candidate only while that moves less
  than 6 px a frame, so a swing of the fan direction near the focus no longer moves it: also M1); it edges up to
  8 px aside when another tag sways into it; it goes back only to one of its preferred places, and only once that has
  been free for 4.5 s; a tag that lost its place shows again only after a new one has been free for 2.5 s. On a boat
  rocking ±0.5° in pitch and ±1.5° in roll at 12 kn: Forces + Wind + X-ray went from 53 changes of place in 12 s to 0,
  with Labels too from 146 to 8 (test limits 6 and 12).
- **I2.** Where no place on the mast shows the whole triangle (the sail view; the helm view on a reach), both
  triangles are drawn as an inset in the top right corner of the safe area, 8 m in front of the camera and about
  150 px across, still in the world's directions. The deck triangle shrinks to fit before it is left out. Tested in
  all five cameras at 45° and 90°.
- **M2** a tag with no room searches again every 0.25 s, not every frame; **M3** the glide now runs headless and is
  tested; **M4** the x-ray backdrop is 500 m across (enough down to a camera 1° above the horizon); **M5** the slice's
  streamline spacing has hysteresis (a camera resting on a step's edge retraced 37 times in 2 s, now at most once);
  **M7** fixed tags are placed before movable ones; **M9** a tag wider or taller than the safe area is hidden;
  **M8** the angle-of-attack arc is drawn from the chord toward the wind by the tag's angle (the effective angle, the
  trim panel's number), so it stops short of the drawn apparent wind by the downwash — about 6° on a beam reach in
  10–12 kn, more than the review's estimate; lesson 5 says so.
- Tests added for lift square to the apparent wind and on the leeward side, the x-ray's track arrow to leeward by the
  leeway angle, and (end to end) lesson 5's 38 m top view and its reset.

Still open:

- From the free camera's default place the masthead triangle is seen nearly edge-on (30–50 px across).
- **Not yet checked:** the x-ray at dusk and on the Low tier, the phone layout with tags, the cost of the SVG leader
  lines in a browser. With every overlay on, `Overlays.update` measured about 1.0 ms median and 1.2–1.7 ms at the
  95th percentile on a loaded machine, against a 1.5 ms budget.

## 3. Clouds (partly needs a GPU)

Done and merged: the review's findings (stale border texels in the strip-wise environment bake, with a check that
now compares against a changed sky; the six literals shared by the CPU mirror and the shader; a real test of the sun
march; an end-to-end check behind `?suncheck=1`; a self-test that falls back to the 2-D layer; tiles per second
rather than per frame; the clear strip at the horizon under a deck; parked panoramas freed on Low).

Still open:

- **Sunshine: done on 2026-10-01** (CPU statistics, `cloudField.test.ts`). Default cover 0.25 (`DEFAULT_CLOUD_COVER`);
  `BEAM_EXTINCTION` 0.6 → 0.15; cloud more than 6 km along the sun's path dims the light at most to 70 %
  (`SUN_REACH_M`, `FAR_CLOUD_SUN`: decided with the user; it only matters for a sun below about 9°); the sun-in-a-gap
  search runs again 0.6 s after the time of day settles. At 12 kn: 13:00 sunny 76 % / shaded 14 %; 17:00 66 % / 22 %;
  18:45 no shade, full sun about 37 % of the time and 70 % light the rest. Shade spells over 4 minutes: about 0.2 per
  hour at 13:00 and 0.6 per hour at 17:00, the longest 10–11 minutes; the user accepted this (real cumulus clusters do
  it) and the target is now "at most about one an hour, none over 12 minutes". Still to do on a GPU: look at the sky at
  cover 0.25, and re-run `e2e/clouds.spec.ts` (at 0.25 its rays are about 47 % cloud, was 58 %; the check needs > 15 %).
- **Volumetric clouds on the Low tier.** Low still shows the painted layer. Add a cheap entry in
  `cloudQualityFor` (about 512², 64² tiles, 24 × 3 steps); keep it only if it costs no more than 0.15 ms per frame
  on Low and looks clearly better. Software renderers keep the painted layer (`volumetricClouds: false`).
- **Cloud shadows on the sea.** Today the whole sea dims at once when a cloud covers the sun at the boat. Wanted: a
  top-down map of the sun's transmittance at sea level (about 256² over 20 km, centred on the camera, snapped to
  texels, amortised like the panorama, using the same reach rule as the sun march), exposed on `SkyState` with the
  sun's intensity without cloud dimming, and used by the sea for its three direct-sun terms (the light scattered by
  the water body, the glitter, the sunlit foam). Budget 0.15 ms per frame on High and one texture lookup in the sea
  shader; a strength control and `?cloudShadows=0` to compare.
- **Costs to re-measure** on a quiet machine: the march after the conservative envelope test, the 11-step bake
  after a change of light, the panorama per tier.

## Decisions taken on 2026-10-01

- The chase and free cameras stand about 19.5 m back at 1440×900 (was 17 m), so that the masthead and the waterline
  both show between the top bar and the wind dial. The learner's zoom overrides it.
- Trim-advice toasts ("Luffing", "Backwinded main") are off while a lesson runs; the step's own text and hints teach.
- A lesson gives the learner's own overlays and camera back when it ends.
- Clicking the Free sail button while already in Free sail still restarts it: it is the only "start over" there.
- With no GPU the app starts on the Low tier with the painted clouds.
